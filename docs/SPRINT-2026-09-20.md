## Worker-size rule of thumb

- **Delegate (qwen3.8):** single-file codegen, config/healthcheck edits, log
  diagnosis, doc snippets, test scaffolding.
- **Delegate (north-mini):** triage + close stale issues, short summaries, labels.
- **Do NOT delegate:** multi-service debugging (#117), anything needing live
  container state or secrets, anything touching `.env`/credentials, final QA.
